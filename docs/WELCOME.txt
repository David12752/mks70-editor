Welcome to MKS-70 Editor!
==========================

Thank you for installing MKS-70 Editor, the professional editor for
Roland MKS-70 and JX-10 synthesizers.


GETTING STARTED
---------------

1. Connect your MKS-70 or JX-10 to your computer via MIDI
2. Launch MKS-70 Editor
3. Go to the Settings tab to configure MIDI input/output ports
4. Click "Request" to fetch current patch data from the synth


FEATURES
--------

* Real-time parameter editing with visual feedback
* Dual-tone editing (Tone A and Tone B)
* DCO, VCF, VCA, and LFO controls
* Envelope editing with visual graphs
* 300+ factory presets organized by category
* Preset librarian with search and AI analysis
* VST3 plugin for DAW integration


PRESETS LOCATION
----------------

Your presets are stored in:
  %APPDATA%\MKS-70 Editor\Presets\

Factory presets are organized by category:
  Bass, Lead, Pad, Keys, Strings, Brass, FX, and more

If you are interested in piano, violin, and woodwind patches............
this collection is NOT what you are looking for.  It is comprised mostly of
layered synth sounds such as soundtracks, background tracks, and synthetic
sound effects.  Some of the more interesting ones showing off the
capabilities of the Super JX are:
A-5, B-2, B-4, C-3, G-2, and E-7.

(Note: C-64 memory cartridge must be installed in Super JX and memory
 protect switch on cartridge turned off to load this GenPatch (Atari ST) file.
 All current patches and tones on C-64 cartridge will be overwritten so be
 sure to backup all information to disk before loading this file!)
